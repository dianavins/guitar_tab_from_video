# guitar_tab_from_video
Makes guitar tabs from videos/audios

(1) Takes in an mp4 video with fingerpicking and singing and extracts the audio
(2) seperates the guitar and singing audios, saves the guitar audio
(3) transcribes the guitar audio into notes (MIDI). Have to go to Musescore after to make sheet music for next step.
(4) turns the notes into guitar TABs
